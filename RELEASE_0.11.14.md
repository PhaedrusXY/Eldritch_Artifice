# Eldritch Artifice 0.11.14 prototype

- Tier 5 Eldrin Altar recipe for the Staff of the Open Way (3000 Ender and 2000 Arcane power).
- Yog-Sothoth drops 64–128 Eldritch Marks and a guaranteed M&A spell-part thesis containing Caged Singularity on player kills. There is also a 25% chance of a Rift Pearl.
- The Caged Singularity tier 5 silver spell creates an anchored pull for 4–16 seconds. Its field affects nearby creatures and players, excludes the caster and the boss, and does no damage. Active fields are capped at 16 and only scan locally every four ticks.
- Codex pages describe the staff recipe and the hidden spell, with the spell thesis usable through the Book of Rote.
- This patch compiles against the prior jar and M&A 3.1.11 with local API stubs. It has not yet been booted on a dedicated server.
